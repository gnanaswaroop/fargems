FarGems

See what is above you, right now: aircraft, satellites, the Sun, Moon, planets, bright stars and meteor showers.

Two permissions

  • Location Works out what is overhead from where you are. Not asked yet
  • Compass Points the sky map the way you face. Optional. Not asked yet

Open the sky view

Location and compass stay on your device. They are used only to calculate positions and to fetch public data for your area.
