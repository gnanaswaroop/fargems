Display

Measured along the ground from you, so a jet overhead counts as 0 km. Starts at 25 km. Raise it to see flights farther away (up to 100 km).

One AU is the average distance from the Earth to the Sun, about 150 million km (93 million miles). Light travel time is how long light takes to reach you: about 8 minutes from the Sun.

Map layers

Turn map layers on or off. The radar sweep turns clockwise, like a radar screen.

Data: adsb.lol, adsb.fi, OpenSky, CelesTrak, Open-Meteo. Calculations run in your browser. Location and compass stay on your device.
