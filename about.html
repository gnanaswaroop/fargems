FarGems

About FarGems

FarGems is a live sky map with an augmented reality (AR) camera view: the aircraft, satellites, Sun, Moon, planets, bright stars and meteor showers above you right now.

How it works

  1. Hold the phone flat. The radar turns as you turn, so N, E, S and W stay true.
  2. Spot something. Only flights you could see by eye are drawn. Tap one for details.
  3. Raise the phone. The augmented reality view labels what you point at.

Where the data comes from

Aircraft: adsb.lol, adsb.fi and OpenSky, with routes from adsbdb and hexdb. Satellites: CelesTrak. Sun, Moon, planets and stars: calculated on your device with astronomy-engine. Clouds: Open-Meteo. Descriptions of objects you pick: Wikipedia.

Frequently asked questions

Is FarGems free?
Yes. There is no account, no payment and no advertising.
Why does FarGems need my location?
To work out where aircraft, satellites, the Moon and planets are from where you stand. FarGems does not collect it: it stays on your device.
How accurate is the compass?
A phone compass is usually 5 to 20 degrees off. FarGems corrects for the difference between magnetic and true north where you are.
What is the AR view?
Augmented reality shows your camera picture with the planes, satellites, planets and Moon labelled where they really are. Raise your phone to the sky, or tap AR. The picture never leaves your phone.
Can I install FarGems as an app?
Yes. Use Install app in the menu on the home page. On iPhone and iPad, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Privacy

  • FarGems does not collect your location. Your location, compass and camera stay on your device, and the camera picture never leaves your phone.
  • To show what is overhead, your device asks public data services (above) for your area. Aircraft requests go through a small relay that rounds the area and stores nothing about you. Those services see the request as any website would, and have their own policies.
  • FarGems is delivered through Cloudflare, which may process standard request data such as your IP address and browser type for delivery, security and traffic statistics, under its own privacy policy.
  • No accounts, no advertising and no tracking cookies.
